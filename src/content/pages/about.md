I'm Jude. I'm thirteen and I photograph wild animals, mostly birds.

I shoot with a Canon and a telephoto lens. Most of what I do is figuring out
where an animal is going to be and then waiting there, which sounds boring and
sometimes is. The pictures I like best are the ones where the animal is doing
something — calling, taking off, going under — rather than just sitting.

The photograph on this page was taken in Alaska, on a ridge above a river.
